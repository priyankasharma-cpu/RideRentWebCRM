Bike and scooter

Assets: bike.webp, scooter.webp, bike-wheel.webp and scooter-wheel.webp.

The vehicle cutouts are AI-assisted edits of reference images supplied by the client in this project chat. The matching standalone wheel textures were AI-generated from those references. Backgrounds were removed, wheel textures were cropped and optimized to WebP, and body masks preserve stationary forks and exhausts. These are edited photorealistic composites, not unedited manufacturer photographs. No third-party stock/manufacturer license is asserted for the supplied references.
